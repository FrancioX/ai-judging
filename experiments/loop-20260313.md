## Iteration 2 — Sweep `of_drift_guard_px`

**Date:** 2026-03-13
**Goal:** Exp 20→21a showed reducing drift guard improved Arno (51.4→47.9px); test tighter values (50, 100, 150) to see if further tightening helps.

**Implementation:** Pure config sweep — no code changes. Values 50, 100, 150, 200 (current) tested on 4-video mini set using `scripts/sweep_drift_guard.py`.

**Results:**

| drift_guard | Arno | Andreas | Jonatan | Quentin | Mean err | HOTA | ms/fr |
|:-----------:|:----:|:-------:|:-------:|:-------:|:--------:|:----:|:-----:|
| 50 | 51.0 | 8.7 | 6.8 | 56.9 | 30.85 | 0.815 | 44.8ms |
| 100 | 50.9 | 8.7 | 4.9 | 54.7 | 29.80 | 0.826 | 45.3ms |

| **200** *(current)* | **47.9** | **8.7** | **4.6** | **47.8** | **27.25** | **0.844** | **46.0ms** |

**Conclusion:** Monotonically worse as drift guard tightens. OF provides better gap estimates than linear interpolation even during aerial phases — falling back to linear is strictly worse. **Rejected — config unchanged.**

**Key insight:** Even with correct OF tracking the aerial path, the skier's actual trajectory during aerials diverges widely from both OF and linear estimates. The 47.9px mean error for Arno is largely from ~713 interpolated frames (50% of video). Pure parameter tuning of gap-fill quality has likely reached its ceiling.

## Iteration 4 — CoTracker3 Phase B.1 Long-Gap Fill

**Date:** 2026-03-13
**Goal:** Replace LK optical flow with CoTracker3's joint offline trajectory model for gaps >= 20 frames, to better handle aerial phases where LK track features.

**Implementation:** New module `src/tracking/cotracker_fill.py` with `fill_long_gaps_cotracker()` called as Phase B.1 after Phase B LK fill. Integration wired into `tracker.py` via `cotracker_enabled` / `cotracker_min_gap` / `cotracker_resize_h` params; config keys added to `config.yaml`. Sweep script: `scripts/sweep_cotracker.py`.

- 10 query points per gap: 5 from anchor-A bbox corners+center at t=0, 5 from anchor-B at t=n-1
- Falls back to LK fill if model unavailable or if fewer than 2 points remain visible at any frame

**Results (partial — sweep terminated early; baseline and CoTracker min_gap=20 confirmed):**

| Config | Arno | Andreas | Jonatan | Quentin | Mean err | HOTA | ms/fr |
|:------:|:----:|:-------:|:-------:|:-------:|:--------:|:----:|:-----:|
| **no CoTracker (baseline)** | **47.9** | **8.7** | **4.6** | **47.8** | **27.25** | **0.844** | **45.9ms** |
| CoTracker min_gap=20 | 47.9 | 8.7 | 4.6 | 47.8 | 27.25 | 0.844 | ~48ms |

**Conclusion:** Completely flat — all qualifying gaps fall back to LK fill ("replaced 0 gap frames, N gap(s) fell back"). Root cause: during aerial phases the visibility score for all 10 tracked points drops below 0.5 (model cannot re-identify skier body points through rotation/occlusion). CoTracker adds latency (~2ms/frame overhead for model loading) with no benefit. **Rejected — config unchanged (cotracker_enabled: false). Code kept for potential future use with a lower min_visible threshold or SAMURAI.**
